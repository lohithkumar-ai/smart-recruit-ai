📌 About the Project
SMART RECRUIT is a frontend recruitment and Applicant Tracking
System (ATS) prototype designed to demonstrate how HR teams could manage
job openings, candidate applications, recruitment stages, interview
scheduling, and AI-assisted screening from one workspace.
The project includes a product overview and a live-demo interface
intended for an HR internship demonstration. It uses simulated candidate
records and recruitment-source data to illustrate the workflow.
> **Prototype disclosure:** This is a demonstration project. LinkedIn,
> Naukri, Indeed, email services, and other external platforms are **not
> represented as connected integrations**. Any external-source
> applications and dashboard metrics shown in the demo are simulated. AI
> screening results are illustrative decision-support examples, not
> validated hiring predictions.

✨ Key Features
The prototype is designed to demonstrate the following capabilities.
Availability of each feature depends on what is implemented in the
current build.
Recruitment dashboard --- sample application, shortlist,
interview, and selection metrics.
Candidate and application management --- review candidate
details, application source, skills, match score, and status.
Recruitment pipeline --- visualize hiring stages such as
Applied, Screening, Shortlisted, Interview, Selected, and Rejected.
Simulated AI screening --- compare example candidate skills with
a job's required skills.
Job management --- showcase job roles and required skills.
Interview management --- demonstrate interview scheduling and
interview details.
Recruitment analytics --- sample source, funnel, and application
metrics.
Email template previews --- illustrate common application and
interview communications.
Duplicate candidate checks --- demonstrate a possible duplicate
warning based on candidate contact details.
Responsive SaaS-style interface --- professional blue/indigo
visual theme, cards, tables, and navigation.
🧭 Recruitment Workflow
``` text
Recruitment Sources (Simulated)
          |
          v
Application Intake / Manual Entry
          |
          v
Candidate & Application Records
          |
          v
Skill-Match Screening Assistance
          |
          v
HR Review and Recruitment Pipeline
          |
          v
Interview -> Selection -> Onboarding
```
The intended architecture can support future authorized integrations
through official APIs or supported import mechanisms. This prototype
does not scrape recruitment websites or claim access to their private
APIs.
🗺️ Three-Month Product Development Vision
This roadmap describes the planned product vision, not a claim that
every item is already implemented.
Month 1 --- Research & Foundation
Study recruitment workflows and repetitive HR tasks.
Gather requirements and define user journeys.
Plan UI/UX, data models, and ATS architecture.
Month 2 --- Core ATS Development
Expand job and candidate management.
Develop resume and application tracking workflows.
Improve recruitment pipeline and interview management.
Add candidate database and duplicate detection.
Month 3 --- AI & Automation
Explore resume parsing and skill extraction.
Develop candidate-to-job matching assistance.
Add recruitment analytics and email-template automation.
Investigate notifications and future authorized integrations.

🔒 Demo Data & Responsible Use
Dashboard figures and candidate records are sample/demo data.
External recruitment platforms are not connected unless a future
version explicitly implements an authorized integration.
AI match scores are illustrative and should not be used as the sole
basis for employment decisions.
Final recruitment decisions should remain with qualified human
reviewers.
Do not upload real candidate personal information to a public demo.
