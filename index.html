The Challenge

Problem Statement

Modern HR teams receive applications from multiple recruitment channels. Managing candidate information, screening resumes, scheduling interviews and tracking recruitment stages manually can consume significant time.

Scattered Applications

Applications scattered across multiple sources — LinkedIn, Naukri, emails, referrals — with no central repository.

Repetitive Resume Screening

HR manually reviews hundreds of resumes to identify relevant candidates, consuming valuable time.

Duplicate Applications

Same candidates apply through multiple channels, creating confusion and redundant processing.

Difficult Tracking

Tracking recruitment stages, interview schedules, and candidate statuses across spreadsheets is error-prone.

Our Approach

Proposed Solution

Smart Recruit provides a centralized recruitment workspace where HR can manage candidates, track hiring stages, use AI-assisted resume matching, schedule interviews and analyze recruitment performance.

Capabilities

Planned Features

A comprehensive suite of tools designed to streamline every stage of the recruitment lifecycle.

Multi-Source Applications

Aggregate applications from LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, company website, referrals and more.

Candidate Management

Centralized candidate profiles with complete application history and communication logs.

Resume Management

Store, organize and quickly retrieve candidate resumes with structured data extraction.

AI-Assisted Screening

Intelligent resume analysis with skill matching and candidate-job compatibility scoring.

Skill Matching

Automated comparison of candidate skills against job requirements with gap analysis.

Duplicate Detection

Automatically identify duplicate applications across multiple channels to prevent redundancy.

Interview Scheduling

Schedule, manage and track interviews with calendar integration and reminders.

Candidate Communication

Automated email templates for application updates, interview invitations and status notifications.

Recruitment Analytics

Visual dashboards with hiring metrics, source analysis and recruitment funnel insights.

Hiring Pipeline

Kanban-style recruitment pipeline with drag-and-drop candidate stage management.

Job Management

Create, publish and manage job postings with detailed requirements and descriptions.

Onboarding Workflow

Streamlined onboarding process management for selected candidates joining the organization.
